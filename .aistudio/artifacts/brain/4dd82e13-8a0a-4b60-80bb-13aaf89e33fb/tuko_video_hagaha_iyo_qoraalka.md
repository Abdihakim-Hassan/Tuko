# 🎬 Hagaha Buuxa ee Qoraalka Codka (Voiceover Script) ee App-ka TUKO

Muuqaalkani waa **Motion Graphic** loogu talagalay in lagu sharxo dhammaan qaybaha muhiimka ah ee app-ka **Tuko**, si dadku u fahmaan sida uu u shaqeeyo iyo sida ay taleefankooda ugu shuban karaan.

---

## ⏱️ Qaybaha Muuqaalka & Qoraalka Codka (Voiceover Script)

### 🌟 QAYBTA 1: Hordhac & Soo Dhawayn (0:00 – 0:08)
- **Muuqaalka Shaashadda**: Astaanta quruxda badan ee Tuko oo ifeysa, oo ay weheliyaan falaaraha iftiinka cagaaran iyo turquoise-ka, iyo qoraalka *“TUKO: App-ka Casriga ah ee Ilaalinta Salaadda & Adkaarta”*.
- **Codka (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Kusoo dhawoow Tuko – App-ka casriga ah ee Soomaaliyeed ee loogu talagalay ilaalinta shanta salaadood, akhriska Qur'aanka Kariimka, adkaarta subax iyo galab, iyo maareynta cibaadooyinkaaga maalinlaha ah!"*

---

### 👤 QAYBTA 2: Diiwaangelinta & Xogta Gaarka ah (0:08 – 0:16)
- **Muuqaalka Shaashadda**: Animation muujinaya sanduuqa diiwaangelinta, doorashada Wiil / Gabar, iyo tirada cibaadooyinka oo ka bilaabanaya eber (`0`).
- **Codka (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Marka aad app-ka soo gasho, waxaad ku samaynaysaa diiwaangelin fudud. Xogtaadu waxay ka bilaabanaysaa eber (0), waxaadna dooranaysaa Wiil ama Gabar si app-ku kuugu habeeyo cibaadooyinka gaarka kuu ah. Qof kale xogtiisa kuma dhex qasanto!"*

---

### 🕌 QAYBTA 3: 5-ta Salaadood & Aadaanka Tooska ah (0:16 – 0:25)
- **Muuqaalka Shaashadda**: Saacadda salaadaha oo countdown toos ah ku jirta (Fajr, Duhur, Casar, Maghrib, Cishaa), magaalooyinka Soomaaliya (Muqdisho, Hargeysa, Garoowe...), iyo liiska codadka aadaanka.
- **Codka (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Ka eego waqtiyada saxda ah ee salaad kasta magaalo kasta oo aad Soomaaliya kaga sugan tahay. App-ku wuxuu si toos ah kuu siinayaa tirada daqiiqadaha kaaga dhiman salaadda xigta, waxaadna ku dhagaysan kartaa Aadaanka nool oo ay aqrinayaan 8 Shiiq oo caan ah!"*

---

### 📿 QAYBTA 4: Qadaa Tracker & 30-Day Khatm Planner (0:25 – 0:34)
- **Muuqaalka Shaashadda**: Qaybta Qadaada oo leh badhanka (+ / -) ee maalmaha soonka iyo salaadaha, iyo jadwalka 30-ka maalmood ee Khatinka Qur'aanka Kariimka.
- **Codka (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Miyaa salaad ama soon ku dhaafay? Tuko waxay kuu xisaabinaysaa tirada maalmaha soonka ama salaadaha qadaada ah ee kugu waajibay, gaar ahaan gabdhaha bisha Ramadaan kadib. Sidoo kale waxaad helaysaa jadwalka 30-ka maalmood ee Khatinka Qur’aanka!"*

---

### 🧭 QAYBTA 5: Adkaarta, Tasbiixda & Jiheeyaha Qiblada (0:34 – 0:42)
- **Muuqaalka Shaashadda**: Jiheeyaha Qiblada 3D-ga ah oo u leexanaya xagga Kacbada, iyo Tasbiixda dijitaalka ah ee gariiraysa taabasho kasta.
- **Codka (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Akhriso adkaarta subax, galab iyo tan hurdada oo Carabi iyo micno Soomaali ah wata. Isticmaal Tasbiixda casriga ah ee gariirka leh, oo soo saar Jiheeyaha Qiblada ee 3D-ga ah si aad u ogaato jihada saxda ah ee Kacbada meel kasta oo aad adduunka ka joogto!"*

---

### ⏰ QAYBTA 6: To-Do List-ka & Alaarm-ka Mobile-ka (0:42 – 0:51)
- **Muuqaalka Shaashadda**: Jadwalka hawlaha maalinlaha ah, saacadda AM/PM ee 12-ka saac ah, countdown timer-ka, iyo badhanka alaarm-ka.
- **Codka (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Qorsheyso hawlahaaga maalinlaha ah adigoo adeegsanaya To-Do List-ka casriga ah. Ku dar saacadda xusuusinta ee Due Time, sawirro, tags gaar ah, oo daji Alaarm-ka Mobile-ka si aadan u seegin Salaadda Tahajjudka ama Fajarka!"*

---

### 📲 QAYBTA 7: Sida Loo Soo Degsado (PWA 1 Gujis) (0:51 – 1:00)
- **Muuqaalka Shaashadda**: Shaashad taleefan oo muujinaysa gujinta badhanka *"Soo Degso App-ka"*, astaanta Tuko oo toos ugu soo dhacaysa shaashadda guriga (Home Screen) ee Android iyo iPhone.
- **Codka (Voiceover)**:
  > *"Tuko uma baahna inaad ka raadiso Play Store ama App Store, wax lacag ahna kuma kacayso! Kaliya ka fur link-ga browser-kaaga, riix 'Soo Dagso App-ka' ama 'Add to Home Screen', wuxuuna kuugu degayaa taleefankaaga sidii app caadi ah!"*
